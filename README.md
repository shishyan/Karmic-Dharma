# 🧞‍♂️ Karmic Dharma (`Dharma-Driven Open Ledger`)

> **"In the Old Economy, status came from how much you hoarded. In the Karmic Genie Economy, status, fame, and civic privilege come ONLY from how much you give away and how much verified Dharma you perform."**

**Karmic Genie** is an open-source, cryptographically verified **Dharma & Generosity Protocol** designed for towns, cities, states, and nations. It combines our **Federated Civic Open Ledger** (Zero-Leakage Public Treasury + 0% Mutual Credit) with a **Proof-of-Dharma (`PoD`) & Soulbound Prestige Engine** that structurally flips human incentives:

1. **You Cannot Buy Status—You Can Only Give It Into Existence:** Wealth hoarding earns **zero** prestige and gradually loses purchasing power via a built-in **Hoarding Demurrage Tax**, while verified giving (*Dāna*) and community service (*Seva*) mint non-transferable **Soulbound Dharma Prestige (`Karmic Aura`)**.
2. **Proof-of-Dharma (`PoD`) Consensus:** Acts of Dharma—funding a school, planting trees, feeding the hungry (*Annadāna*), forgiving a neighbor's debt, building public water infrastructure, or teaching skills (*Vidyādāna*)—are cryptographically verified by the beneficiaries and a local Citizen Jury before minting Karma.
3. **The Inverse-Hoarding Multiplier:** Giving away 10% of a modest fortune earns higher Karmic velocity than giving 0.01% of a billionaire's hoard, forcing the ultra-wealthy to compete in **massive, verifiable public generosity** if they want social standing, honorific titles, and civic influence.

## ⚖️ The 4 Core Laws of the Karmic Genie Protocol

### Law 1: Soulbound Dharma Prestige (`Non-Transferable Reputation`)
Every participant has two balances on the ledger:
1. **Material Balance (`1:1 Sovereign Currency + 0% Mutual Credit`):** Used for everyday trade and livelihood.
2. **Soulbound Karma (`Dharma Points & Civic Tier`):** Cannot be bought, sold, or transferred. It is minted **only** when a citizen performs verified *Dāna* (giving to the commons/needy) or *Seva* (verified community labor/service).

### Law 2: The 5 Pillars of Verified Dharma (*Pancha-Dāna*)
Karmic Genie categorizes and weights contributions across five classical and civic pillars:
- 🍲 **Annadāna & Aarogya (Food & Healthcare):** Funding community kitchens, nutrition, and free medical care (`1.5x Karma Multiplier`).
- 📚 **Vidyādāna (Open Knowledge & Education):** Teaching, funding schools, open-sourcing tools (`1.4x Karma Multiplier`).
- 🌱 **Bhumi & Prana Seva (Ecological & Civic Infrastructure):** Planting trees, clean water plants, solar microgrids, road maintenance (`1.6x Karma Multiplier`).
- 🤝 **Runa-Vimochana (Debt Relief & 0% Credit Backing):** Backing interest-free mutual credit for local farmers/artisans or forgiving hardship debt (`1.8x Karma Multiplier`).
- ⚖️ **Satya & Nyaya Seva (Civic Audit & Justice):** Serving honestly on local Citizen Juries to verify public works (`1.3x Karma Multiplier`).

### Law 3: Anti-Hoarding Velocity & The "Golden Potlatch" Effect
If an account hoards vast idle wealth above the **Community Abundance Threshold** without circulating it in local trade, backing 0% mutual credit, or performing *Dāna*:
- Its **Karmic Aura decays** every epoch, visibly dropping the holder's social rank on the Public Town Leaderboard.
- An automatic **Hoarding Demurrage (e.g., 2% per quarter on idle mega-hoards)** recycles dormant capital directly into the **Town Commons Pool**.
- Conversely, transferring wealth directly into verified public works or citizen relief converts decaying hoard into **Immortal Civic Prestige**.

### Law 4: Dharma Honour Tiers (`Samaj-Gaurav`)
Citizens and enterprises ascend public, verifiable tiers of respect based on their cumulative and active Dharma:
1. 🌱 **Sevak (Initiate):** Active contributor to local mutual aid.
2. 🔥 **Dharma-Rakshak (Community Guardian):** Consistently gives >10% of surplus or 10+ hours/month of verified Seva.
3. 🏛️ **Lok-Stambha (Pillar of the Town):** Funded or built major public infrastructure, zero-interest credit pools, or schools.
4. 👑 **Mahādānī / Rajarshi (Supreme Benefactor):** Gave away the majority of accumulated fortune to elevate the entire town, city, or nation.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Launch the Interactive Web App & Government Simulator
Simply open `index.html` in any modern browser, or serve it locally:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
# Open http://localhost:8080 in your browser
```

### 2. Run the Python CLI Karmic Ledger Engine
Run the standalone SHA-256 Proof-of-Dharma blockchain simulation in your terminal:

```bash
python3 karmic_engine.py
```
